Week at a glance
Mon, 25 May In-person
Kickoff
Opening speech by Dir, sharing of the problem statements. Teams to confirm their choice, then start building!
Opening by Director — 9.00am
Q&A on problem statements — teams ask questions and confirm their choice
Zenmux setup demo — connect Claude Desktop to your API key
LangGraph setup — walkthrough and environment setup
Start building — afternoon
Tue, 26 May
Build day
A full day to work on your solution. Experiment with prompting approaches and divide work across the team.
Wed, 27 May Public holiday
Rest day
No deliverables today. Use the time to experiment freely, explore prompting approaches, or simply take a break.
Thu, 28 May
Build day & final prep
Final day to build and polish. Wrap up your solution and prepare your demo for the sharing session tomorrow.
Fri, 29 May — 9.30am Final
Virtual sharing session via Skype
Present your solution to all groups. Each team demos their build and shares a short reflection: what AI helped with, where it fell short, and what you'd do differently.

Three problem statements are available in the Resources section below. Each team picks one to work on — first to confirm in the room gets it. If there's a clash, teams are encouraged to negotiate. You may also counter-propose your own problem statement for approval.

API credits

Each team receives a fixed credit allocation via Zenmux. Treat this as part of the challenge — efficient, well-structured prompts go further. If your team runs low, review your prompting approach before requesting more.
