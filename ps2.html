Problem Statement 02

Eliminate Engineering Toil in C3 Workflows

Make best practices the path of least resistance — structurally embed good engineering into the tools C3 teams already reach for.

Background

C3 development teams routinely absorb hours in repetitive, low-value tasks — redundant environment setups, boilerplate configurations, inconsistent code scaffolding, and copy-paste debugging steps — that quietly drain productive capacity without ever appearing on a risk register. Best practices exist and are well understood, yet they are inconsistently followed because the path of least resistance rarely aligns with the path of best practice. Following standards requires conscious effort; cutting corners is simply faster in the moment.

The real opportunity is not to remind engineers what good looks like, but to structurally embed it into the tools they already reach for — so that best practices happen as a natural consequence of the workflow, not as an act of discipline.

Your mission

Identify a specific source of engineering toil within C3 development workflows and design a prototype that makes the correct approach the easiest approach. Your solution should reduce or eliminate the identified toil through automation, intelligent scaffolding, or workflow integration — and demonstrate that best practice adherence becomes the default, not the exception.

You do not need to boil the ocean. Pick one well-defined pain point and solve it meaningfully rather than sketching a broad platform that addresses everything shallowly.

Suggested starting points — you are not limited to these
Accessible
Use an AI coding assistant to generate and validate boilerplate configurations for a specific C3 module (e.g. an incident management service), and reflect on how it reduced setup time and improved consistency.
Build an AI-assisted onboarding guide that answers common C3 development questions conversationally, reducing reliance on tribal knowledge and undocumented institutional practices.
Intermediate
Build an AI-powered tool that scans a C3 codebase for deviations from GC3 architectural standards — such as inconsistent module boundaries or missing governance hooks — and surfaces prioritised recommendations.
Design an intelligent scaffolding tool that generates new C3-compliant service templates from a natural language description, ensuring architectural consistency across the product family from the start.
Advanced
Implement an agentic code review assistant trained on C3 engineering standards that intercepts pull requests, identifies toil patterns and standard deviations, and proposes concrete remediations before human review.
Build an MCP-connected developer agent that monitors C3 pipeline activity in real-time, proactively surfaces emerging toil patterns across the team, and auto-generates fixes or templates for repeated issues.
