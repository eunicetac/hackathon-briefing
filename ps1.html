Problem Statement 01

Next-Generation GC3 Foundation Platform

Build the AI-enabled platform layer that product teams use to create derivative C3 applications — not an end-user tool, but the foundation everything else is built on.

Background

Operators managing incidents and tasking today are constrained by rigid, pre-scripted workflows that cannot adapt to evolving situations. This leads to delayed decisions, fragmented tool coordination, and over-reliance on manual intervention for routine orchestration tasks. Current Command, Control and Communications (C3) systems struggle to handle data ambiguity, cascade failures, or rapidly shifting operational priorities.

The Generic C3 (GC3) platform aims to establish a foundation of common, reusable capabilities for C3 functions. The central question this hackathon explores is: how might AI reimagine this foundation as a dynamic, self-correcting orchestration engine that accelerates decision-making while keeping humans firmly in control?

GC3 is not an end-user application. It is a foundation platform consumed by product teams who build derivative C3 products on top of it. The measure of a good GC3 platform is not how well it serves operators directly, but how easily and consistently product teams can extend it to build capabilities that do.

Your mission

Design and develop a prototype of an AI-enabled GC3 foundation platform that product teams can build on. Your prototype should demonstrate at least one common module — such as incident management or map services — exposed as a reusable, extensible building block. A strong submission will show not just what the platform does, but how easily another team could build on top of it — through clear APIs, consistent patterns, and sensible defaults that make the right way the easy way.

You do not need to boil the ocean. Pick one well-defined module and demonstrate it meaningfully rather than sketching a broad platform that addresses everything shallowly.

Suggested starting points — you are not limited to these
Accessible
Use an AI coding assistant to scaffold a modular GC3 service with clean API boundaries, and reflect on how it accelerated development and enforced consistency.
Build a simple AI-assisted onboarding experience for a GC3 common module — helping a new product team understand available capabilities and how to integrate them through natural language interaction.
Intermediate
Design an AI-enabled incident management module exposed as a clean API, with an agentic layer that handles workflow orchestration and surfaces structured outputs for consuming product teams.
Build a GC3 service registry where product teams can discover, compose, and configure available common modules, with an AI assistant that recommends appropriate modules based on a natural language description of their product need.
Advanced
Implement a multi-agent orchestration backbone where product teams can declaratively define workflows composed of GC3 common modules, and the platform dynamically resolves, sequences, and monitors execution at runtime.
Build an AI-powered GC3 SDK that generates product-team-facing scaffolding from a natural language brief — producing correctly wired, standards-compliant service integrations that consume GC3 common modules out of the box.
